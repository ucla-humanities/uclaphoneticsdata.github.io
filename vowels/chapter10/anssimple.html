The five pairs of sounds:

1. Same (Sound1 at the beginning of both) --[hissbuzzhissbuzz]

2. Same (Sound 2 at the beginning of both) -- [buzzhissbuzzhiss]

3. Different (Sound 1 at the beginning of the first and end of the second) - [hissbuzzbuzzhiss]

4. Different (Sound 1 at the end of the first and beginning of the second) - [buzzhisshissbuzz]

5. Same (Sound 2 at beginning of both) - [buzzhissbuzzhiss]
